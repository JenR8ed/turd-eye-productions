# Release Checklist

Use before every upload to DistroKid or other distributor.

- [ ] Track was generated on a paid commercial-rights plan
- [ ] Generation date and plan recorded in rights-log
- [ ] Light human editing performed (cuts / arrangement / cleanup)
- [ ] No real-artist voice or distinctive style cloning
- [ ] Filename is clean and professional
- [ ] Metadata is accurate (title, artist name, genre)
- [ ] AI disclosure will be completed correctly on upload
- [ ] Audio is normalized and free of obvious artifacts
- [ ] Backup of original + edited files kept

**Only proceed when all boxes are checked.**
