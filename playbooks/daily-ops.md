# Daily Ops Playbook

## Minimum Viable Daily Routine (30–60 min)

1. Check for new custom commission messages / orders.
2. Generate 3–8 new tracks (or variations) in your chosen niche(s).
3. Light-edit the best 1–3.
4. Log rights for anything new.
5. Upload ready tracks to DistroKid (if any).
6. Reply to clients or update gig status.

## Weekly Additions
- Review which tracks/styles are getting streams or inquiries.
- Update samples on Fiverr/portfolio.
- Check tool pricing or policy changes (update `configs/tools.json` if needed).

## Monthly
- Calculate actual spend vs income.
- Decide whether to scale custom work, catalog, or YouTube.
