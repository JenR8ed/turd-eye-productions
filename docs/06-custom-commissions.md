# 06 — Custom Commissions (Fastest Path to Profit)

This is the primary recommended profit engine.

## Why It Works
- Clients pay for speed + fit, not streams.
- You keep high margins.
- Catalog builds in the background.

## Pricing Guide (USD)
| Package          | Price     | Includes                              |
|------------------|-----------|---------------------------------------|
| Starter          | $75–120   | 1 track, 1 revision, personal/web use |
| Standard         | $150–250  | 1 track + stems, 2 revisions, commercial use |
| Pro / Brand      | $300–600+ | Multiple versions, priority, exclusivity option |

## Ideal Clients
- YouTubers / podcasters needing beds & intros
- Indie game developers
- Small brands / coaches
- People wanting personal songs (birthdays, proposals, etc.)

## Process
1. Client fills brief (use `templates/client-brief.md`)
2. Generate 2–3 options on commercial plan
3. Light edit best one
4. Deliver + offer paid revision if needed
5. Log the rights

## Platforms to Sell On
- Fiverr
- Upwork
- Your own site / Linktree
- Direct outreach to creators

See `templates/fiverr-gig.md` for ready-to-use copy.
