# 05 — Risk Mitigation

## Legal / Rights Risks
- Only generate on paid commercial plans.
- Log: tool + plan + exact date/time of generation.
- Add human editing (arrangement, cuts, overdubs, original lyrics).
- Prefer lower-risk tools (Soundraw, ElevenLabs, Stable Audio) for client work.

## Platform Policy Risks
- Always disclose AI on DistroKid and when required by DSPs.
- Never mass-upload near-identical or low-effort tracks.
- Avoid voice clones of real artists.
- Monitor: Tidal (no royalties on full AI), Deezer (aggressive detection), Spotify spam filters.

## Quality & Competition Risks
- Specialize in 1–2 niches instead of generic content.
- Treat AI as first draft only.
- Build a consistent aesthetic / brand.

## Business Risks
- Start with one primary generator + DistroKid.
- Keep simple spreadsheet or rights-log for every track.
- Diversify income: custom commissions + catalog + YouTube.
- Have an exit path if a tool’s terms change.

## Red Flags — Stop Immediately
- Tool no longer grants commercial rights on your plan.
- Platform starts removing your tracks for “inauthentic content”.
- Client asks for a specific real artist’s voice/style.
