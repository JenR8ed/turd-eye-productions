# Cost Analysis & Profit Numbers (USD)

## Monthly Burn Rate

- **Minimal viable**: Suno Pro or Soundraw (~$10–15) + DistroKid (~$2/mo) → **$12–17/month**
- **Solid catalog**: Suno Pro ($10) + Soundraw ($15) + DistroKid → **$25–30/month**
- **Higher volume / client-ready**: $40–70/month

**First month total**: ~$35 (tools + distributor)

## Realistic Income Paths

| Path                        | Songs / Content Needed          | Realistic Monthly Income (after 2–4 months) | Notes |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|-------|
| Streaming only              | 80–150+ songs                 | $20 – $150                                  | Slow. Needs volume + playlists |
| YouTube music channel       | 30–60 solid videos            | $50 – $400                                  | Better with original visuals |
| Custom commissions          | 5–10 sample tracks            | $300 – $1,500+                              | Fastest path to profit. $75–250 per job |
| Hybrid (recommended)        | 40–60 released + samples      | $400 – $2,000                               | Best balance |

## Break-even Notes

- Streaming alone rarely profits quickly.
- 5–8 custom jobs/month at $100–150 each = real profit while the catalog builds.
- Keep records of generation date + active plan for rights.
