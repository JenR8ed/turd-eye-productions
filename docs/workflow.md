# Starter Workflow & Process

## Core Pipeline

1. **Generate** on a paid commercial plan (Suno Pro recommended for full songs).
2. **Edit** — cut weak sections, arrange structure, light mix/master (Audacity free or Reaper).
3. **Distribute** via DistroKid (disclose AI use).
4. **Monetize actively** — offer custom commissions while the catalog grows.

## Step-by-Step for First Profit

1. Subscribe to Suno Pro ($10/mo).
2. Generate 10–15 sample tracks in your target niches (sleep, lo-fi, cinematic, podcast beds, etc.).
3. Edit the best 5–8 into clean samples.
4. Create a simple Fiverr/Upwork gig or portfolio page offering custom AI-assisted tracks.
5. Release the edited tracks via DistroKid.
6. Start pitching or listing custom work.
7. Keep generating and releasing consistently (aim for volume in one or two niches).

## Rules to Follow

- Generate only while subscribed for commercial rights.
- Disclose AI on uploads.
- Add human editing for better quality and stronger position.
- Focus custom sales for faster cash flow.
- Avoid voice cloning of real artists and mass spam uploads.
